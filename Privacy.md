# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **PromptCanvas** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our diagramming portal, software, and AI-powered generation services located at **https://promptcanvas-production-235c.up.railway.app** (the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide and improve our AI diagramming services:

*   **Account & Profile Data:** If you register for an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.
*   **Input Data (Prompts):** We collect the business use cases, technical prompts, architectural requirements, and structural text you input into the Service to generate diagrams.
*   **Usage & Device Data:** We automatically collect standard log data when you visit the Service, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction metrics (e.g., which diagram languages or export formats you use most frequently).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following core purposes:
*   **To Provide the Service:** Specifically, to process your text prompts and business use cases through our AI engines and layout algorithms to render diagrams (e.g., Mermaid, PlantUML, Draw.io formats).
*   **To Maintain the Infrastructure:** To monitor the health, security, and performance of our hosting environment.
*   **To Improve the Platform:** To analyze usage trends and optimize our prompt-parsing and diagram-rendering logic. 

## 3. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Processing

Because PromptCanvas relies on AI models to translate text into visual architectures, your data interacts with external AI providers:
*   **Data Transmission:** Your Input Data (prompts and use cases) is transmitted to third-party Large Language Model (LLM) APIs to generate the underlying diagram code.
*   **No Public Training:** We configure our enterprise AI API integrations so that your private prompts and business logic are **not** used by third-party providers to train their public foundation models. 
*   **Hosting:** The Service is hosted on Railway. Your data is stored securely in accordance with standard cloud infrastructure practices.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data or business use cases. We only share information in the following circumstances:
*   **Service Providers:** With cloud hosting providers (e.g., Railway) and API partners strictly for the purpose of operating the Service.
*   **Legal Compliance:** If required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of PromptCanvas, our users, or the public.
*   **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as a business asset.

## 5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your Input Data and Generated Outputs. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We retain your prompts and generated diagrams only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. You may delete your diagrams or account at any time.

## 6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
*   Access the personal information we hold about you.
*   Request correction of inaccurate data.
*   Request deletion of your personal data and account history.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for professionals and enterprise use. It is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
